## 🌾 The Agri-Commerce Crisis & Our Mission

In the conventional agricultural distribution chain, smallholder farmers are trapped in a multi-tier brokerage web:
- **Brokerage Drain**: Commission agents (*arhtiyas*), mandi intermediaries, secondary transporters, and retail aggregators take up to **60%–70% of end-consumer value**.
- **Post-Harvest Wastage**: High transit latency results in drastic spoilage of perishable greens and fruits before reaching metropolitan markets.
- **Opacity**: Farmers lack real-time price transparency and often sell below fair cost-of-production.

**FarmersDirect breaks this bottleneck:**
A unified digital platform connecting certified farmers directly with housing societies, restaurants, and bulk consumer communities, providing **algorithmic demand aggregation**, **fair escrow payouts**, and **hyperlocal dispatch routing**.
