# 🌾 FarmersDirect — System Operational Overview

## Core System Modules

### 1. The Digital Mandi (Catalog & Farmgate Listing)
- Standardized grading metrics (Size, Moisture, Organic Certification).
- Dynamic price advisory utilizing public Agmarknet indices to ensure competitive yet profitable pricing for farmers.

### 2. Hyperlocal Order Aggregation
- Batching orders within a designated geo-fence to minimize per-kilogram logistics overhead.
- Community buying pools enable apartment complexes to share cold-chain delivery slots.

### 3. Transparent Financial Escrow
- Zero predatory broker cuts.
- Direct digital bank transfer (UPI / IMPS) triggered instantly upon delivery acknowledgment.
